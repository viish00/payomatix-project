SUPPORT PLAYBOOK

Help centre

Short, practical guidance for common payment questions.

↗

UPI debit, order failed

Do not ask the customer to pay again immediately. First check transaction status and trace the UTR/RRN.

  • Confirm amount, time and VPA.
  • Check the bank response and merchant callback.
  • For specified failed UPI cases, RBI TAT may be T+1 or T+5 depending on the failure type.
Reference: UTR / RRN
↩

Card refund not received

Confirm the refund was submitted and accepted before giving the customer a timeline.

  • Open the original card transaction.
  • Check refund status and acquirer response.
  • Share the ARN so the card issuer can trace a processed refund.
Reference: ARN
₹

Settlement amount mismatch

Gross sales and net settlement can differ because of adjustments and cut-off timing.

  • Compare the same business date and batch.
  • Review refunds, disputes, fees, taxes and holds.
  • Share an itemised settlement breakdown.
Reference: Settlement ID + breakdown
✓

Support response checklist

A good support reply is calm, specific and traceable.

  • Acknowledge the issue politely.
  • State what you are checking.
  • Share the correct reference ID.
  • Give a realistic next step; never invent a completion time.
Principle: Verify before promise
